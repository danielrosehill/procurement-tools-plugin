## How to read this

- **Status** — `live` (available, meets spec), `excluded` (fails a must-have or hard no — reason given), `discontinued` (existed, no longer sold), `announced` (not shipping yet), `unverified` (found, not yet confirmed against a primary source).
- **Verified** — the date the entry was last checked against a primary source, and which one. An entry with no date is a rumour, not a finding.
- Excluded and discontinued entries **stay in the table**. The shape of what isn't available is half the map.
