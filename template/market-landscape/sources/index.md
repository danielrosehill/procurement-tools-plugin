# Sources

*Every source read, with the date. An undated survey can't be refreshed.*

**Primary** — manufacturer pages, spec sheets, manuals, standards bodies, regulatory filings. Can support a claim.
**Secondary** — reviews, roundups, forum threads, retailer listings. Can surface a name; cannot settle a spec.

Roundups and "best X of 2026" listicles are recall tools. Use them to find options, then verify each against a primary source before it earns a dossier in `candidates/`.
